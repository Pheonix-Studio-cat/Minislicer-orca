Minislicer Mobile and Minislicer Runner
=======================================

Minislicer Mobile and the Minislicer Runner are part of Minislicer-orca, a fork of
OrcaSlicer, and are licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0
(see LICENSE.txt, or https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).

Source code (AGPL-3.0, section 13): https://github.com/Pheonix-Studio-cat/Minislicer-orca
The mobile slicer and the runner live in the folder "mobile/".

The printer, process and filament settings in profiles.json are generated from
OrcaSlicer's Bambu Lab profiles (resources/profiles/BBL in the same repository).
The icons in assets/icons are OrcaSlicer's (resources/images), and the printer
pictures and bed models in assets/printers and assets/beds are the ones OrcaSlicer
ships in resources/profiles/BBL. They are copied unchanged by
mobile/tools/build_profiles.py and fall under the same AGPL-3.0 licence as the
rest of the repository.

The translations in i18n/ are taken from OrcaSlicer's translation catalogs
(localization/i18n in the same repository, AGPL-3.0) where OrcaSlicer has the
same English text. The remaining strings were machine-translated for this
project (mobile/tools/i18n_extra.json); each catalog lists them under
"_meta.machine_translated".

"Bambu Lab" and "Bambu Connect" are names of Bambu Lab. This project is not made,
endorsed or supported by Bambu Lab; the names only describe compatibility.
